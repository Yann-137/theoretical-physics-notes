# Theoretical Physics Notes

A collection of rigorous, technical, and self-contained pedagogical manuscripts on various topics in theoretical physics.

## Published Papers / Notes
1. **[An Introduction to the Born-Oppenheimer Approximation](./Born-Oppenheimer/)**
   - *Topics:* Adiabatic theorem, Berry connection, nuclear covariant derivatives, Born-Huang effective potentials, energy level crossing, and diabatic transformations.
   - [Read PDF](./Born-Oppenheimer/Born-Oppenheimer.pdf)

2. **[Path Integral Quantization for the Gauge Field of Quantum Electrodynamics](./path-integral/)**
   - *Topics:* Green functions, path integral formulation of quantum mechanics, path integral for scalar fields (Klein-Gordon), gauge redundancy, Faddeev-Popov methodology principles, generating functionals, and the derivation of the photon two-point correlation function.
   - [Read PDF](./path-integral/Path-integral-em-gauge-field.pdf)
