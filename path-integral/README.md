# Path Integral Quantization for the Gauge Field of Quantum Electrodynamics

- **Compiled PDF:** [Path-integral-em-gauge-field.pdf](./Path-integral-em-gauge-field.pdf) *(Click to view directly in your browser)*

## Abstract
The canonical quantization of quantum fields is usually the primary technique that one learns when studying quantum field theory for the first time. 
It is relatively straightforward for scalar, vector, and even spinor fields, yet becomes arguably complex for gauge fields because of their inherent internal redundancy. 
This paper provides a detailed and complete derivation of the path integral quantization for the gauge field of quantum electrodynamics. 
We start with the fundamental concept of Green functions, and then derive the path integral formulation of quantum mechanics. 
The path integral approach to quantum fields is presented next, followed by its application to the Klein-Gordon scalar field. Finally, the last section shows how to derive the photon two-point correlation function from the electromagnetic field generating functional.

## Contents Covered
1. Review of Green functions in quantum mechanics
2. Path integral formulation of quantum mechanics
3. Field quantization and application to the Klein-Gordon scalar field
4. Gauge redundancy and QED field quantization
5. Generating functionals and deriving the photon two-point correlation function (Feynman propagator)
